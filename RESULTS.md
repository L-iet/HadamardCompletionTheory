## Exact search in the residual dimension

A $q$-dimensional subspace is determined by $q$ suitable coordinate positions. Consequently all its sign vectors can be found by checking at most $2^q$ assignments, or $2^{q-1}$ after identifying a vector with its negative when $q>0$. For rational linear constraints, the reconstruction and tests use exact rational arithmetic. The exponential cost depends on the residual dimension rather than the ambient matrix order.

## Additional proved results

- General span-rigidity criteria that exclude bounded-cost augmenting exchanges after tensoring.
- Exact candidate enumeration using a number of coordinates equal to the dimension of the residual space.
- Integer and rational reconstruction, modular lifting formulas, and explicit finite repair examples.
- Linear-kernel tests and classifications of real and fourth-root completion problems.
- Unconditional cyclic coefficient constructions from fixed-sum square representations and quadratic characters.
